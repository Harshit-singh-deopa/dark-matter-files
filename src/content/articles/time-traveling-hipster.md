Buried in a photo of a 1940s public gathering, one figure stands out from the rest of the crowd — sunglasses, a modern jacket, and a compact camera unlike anything else in frame.

## The Photograph

The image surfaced from an archival collection decades after it was taken. Everyone else in frame wears clothing typical of the era; one young man, positioned near the center, does not.

## Explanations Offered

Several mundane explanations have been proposed over the years — an early prototype of folding sunglasses, a locally-tailored jacket ahead of mainstream fashion — but none have been confirmed, and the man in question has never been identified.
