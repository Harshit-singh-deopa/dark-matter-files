# **Final Conclusion**

The Greenbrier Ghost is one of the rare historical cases in which a **documented criminal investigation became inseparable from a supernatural legend**.

Zona Heaster Shue was a real woman.

She died in Greenbrier County in 1897.

Her initial death investigation failed to identify the fatal injuries.

Her mother, Mary Jane Heaster, later claimed that Zona appeared to her repeatedly and revealed that her husband had killed her.

The authorities reopened the case.

Zona's body was exhumed.

The medical examination found a dislocated neck and damage to the windpipe.

Edward "Trout" Shue was arrested and ultimately convicted of first-degree murder.

The ghost story nevertheless became inseparably connected with the investigation.
