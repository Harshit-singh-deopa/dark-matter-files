## **The Case at a Glance**

On February 15, 2023, a Ukrainian military **Bayraktar TB2 unmanned aerial vehicle** recorded an unusual object with its infrared/thermal imaging system.

The object appeared as a small, dark, vertically oriented or cigar-shaped form moving rapidly across the scene. According to the later Ukrainian analysis, the object appeared to travel in a straight trajectory, showed no obvious visible propulsion system, and did not appear to accelerate or maneuver during the recorded sequence.

The Ukrainian research organization **SRCAA “Zond”** later examined the footage together with documents from Ukrainian military units. Its analysis concluded that the object could not be satisfactorily identified using the hypotheses available in its identification system and assigned it a **D1 classification**, meaning an unidentified phenomenon with a moderate level of unusualness and information content.

The case was subsequently discussed in the **Spring 2026 special issue of the U.S. Naval Postgraduate School's Combating Threats Exchange (CTX)**. There, researchers Artem Bilyk and Kyrylo Nikolaiev described it as a **single ultra-fast, near-vertical cigar-shaped UAP** recorded by a Bayraktar UAV in central Ukraine. They listed an estimated size of **0.3–0.5 meters**, velocity of approximately **250 m/s**, and altitude of approximately **200–300 meters**.

The important distinction is that **UAP means unidentified aerial phenomenon**. It does not mean alien spacecraft.
