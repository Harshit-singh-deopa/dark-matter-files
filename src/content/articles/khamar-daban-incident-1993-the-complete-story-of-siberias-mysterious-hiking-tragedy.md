# **Khamar-Daban Incident (1993): The Complete Story**

## **Introduction**

On **5 August 1993**, six members of a seven-person hiking group died in the **Khamar-Daban mountain range in Buryatia, southern Siberia**, not far from Lake Baikal.

The group was led by experienced hiking instructor **Lyudmila Korovina**, a 41-year-old master of sport in tourism from Kazakhstan.

The only survivor was **17-year-old Valentina Utochenko**.

What makes the incident extraordinary is the survivor's account of the final hours. According to Utochenko's later testimony, the group suddenly began experiencing severe physical and behavioral problems. One member collapsed, others became confused or apparently panicked, and several died within a short period.

The bodies were eventually recovered from the mountain.

The official forensic conclusion attributed five deaths to **hypothermia**, while Korovina's death was attributed to a **heart attack**. However, the unusual circumstances have generated decades of speculation about poisoning, altitude illness, infrasound, extreme weather, exhaustion and other explanations.

According to accounts from people who knew her, she had traveled through areas including the **Tien Shan, Altai, Sayan Mountains, Mountain Shoria, Karakum and Polar Urals**.
