A government survey crew logged an unidentified shape surfacing near the rocks off the coast, photographed only once before it vanished beneath the swell.

## The Encounter

The crew was conducting a routine coastal survey when several members reported an unusually large shape breaching near a reef formation. One photograph was taken before the object submerged and did not resurface.

## Official Record

The incident was logged only as an "unidentified marine specimen" in the accompanying field notes, and the file was sealed shortly afterward. No follow-up survey of the area was conducted.
