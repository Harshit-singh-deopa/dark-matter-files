About Dark Matter Files

Dark Matter Files

Dark Matter Files collects and preserves reports of paranormal encounters, unsolved disappearances, UFO sightings, unexplored ocean phenomena, and unexplained corners of science and history — submitted by contributors and documented for the curious and the unconvinced alike.

Every case file is presented as submitted: sourced, dated, and left for you to judge. Dark Matter Files does not verify, endorse, or debunk the claims within its pages. It exists to document, not to conclude.

Have a case of your own? Use the report form on the homepage, or reach out through the Feedback link below.
